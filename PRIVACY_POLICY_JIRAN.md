# 📖 Introduction

Jiran ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information and maintaining transparency regarding how your data is handled.

This Privacy Policy applies to:

- 📱 The Jiran mobile application (customer and service provider in one app)
- 🌐 Services provided through `app.jiranapp.net`
- 🔌 Real-time order and chat services on `socket.jiranapp.net`
- 🛠 Related support and operational services

The App is intended for use in **Saudi Arabia**. You choose a role at onboarding: **customer** (request a service) or **provider** (receive and fulfill requests). Guest browsing uses the customer experience until you sign in.

If you do not agree with this policy, please discontinue use of the App.

# 📥 Information We Collect

## 👤 Personal Information

We may collect the following information directly from you:

### 📝 Account Registration
- Full name
- Phone number
- Country code
- Account role (customer or service provider)
- Profile photo

### 🔐 Authentication
- OTP verification data
- Session authentication tokens (stored in secure storage on your device)
- Optional biometric login preference

> ⚠️ We do **not** use password-based authentication.  
> ⚠️ Face ID and fingerprint templates stay on your device. We do **not** upload biometric templates to our servers. Device information is used only to bind that local login to this installation.

### 🧑‍🔧 Provider Profile
If you join as a service provider, we may also collect:

- Professional bio and service details
- Pricing unit (hour, visit, or job)
- Certificates and approval documents
- Portfolio images
- Working hours and availability
- Account approval status (approved, under review, or rejected)

### 📍 Address & Location Information
- Service address and map pin you select when booking
- GPS coordinates used to show nearby providers
- Provider location used when you turn on availability to receive orders

### 📅 Order Information
- Instant or scheduled bookings
- Service category and details
- Notes and instructions
- Price set when a provider accepts
- Order status and history
- Invoices
- Payment method choice (wallet or electronic)

### 💬 Chat
- Messages, read state, and typing signals for an order
- Chat is available from `ready_to_start` until the order is completed or cancelled

### ⭐ Ratings & Feedback
- Ratings you leave for a provider
- Reasons given for a poor review
- Provider review appeals or grievances

### 🛠 Support Requests
- Complaint or suggestion text
- Attached images
- Ticket history and admin replies

### 💳 Wallet & Payment Information
- Wallet balance and transaction history
- Bank name and bank account details you save for withdrawals

Payments made electronically are processed by a third-party payment provider.

> 🔒 We do **not** store full payment card details on our servers.

### ⭐ Favorites
- Providers you save to favorites (customer role)

# 📍 Location Information

We may collect GPS location data when you:

- Pick a service location on the map
- Browse nearby providers
- Enable provider availability so you can receive orders

Location is used for booking, discovery, and fulfillment. You can turn location access off in your device settings. Some features will not work without it.
