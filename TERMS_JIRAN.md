## Description of Service

Jiran is a mobile application that connects customers who need a home service with service providers who fulfill that request. The App provides:

- Role selection (customer or provider) and phone-based registration with OTP verification
- Profile management, including an optional profile photo
- Guest browsing of the customer home until sign-in
- Search and filters for services, categories, and providers
- Provider profiles, portfolios, working hours, certificates, ratings, and a map of nearby providers
- Favorites (customer)
- Instant or scheduled bookings with a map-selected service location
- Order tracking from placement through completion
- Payment by wallet or electronic payment after a provider accepts and sets a price
- Order chat between the customer and the provider on that order
- Ratings of completed services, and provider appeals of a review
- Push notifications for order and account updates
- Wallet, bank account details for withdrawals, complaints, and contact us
- Provider onboarding: professional profile, documents, approval, availability, and order actions

Customers create orders. Providers do not create bookings for a customer. Jiran coordinates the booking. The provider performs the service.

## Bookings and Payments

### Placing Orders

- A customer creates an order by choosing a service, location, and either an instant or a scheduled visit. The order starts as `under_review`
- A provider accepts or rejects that order. Acceptance sets the price and moves the order to `payment_pending`
- The customer pays in the App (wallet or electronic payment). After successful payment the order becomes `ready_to_start`, and order chat opens
- The provider then marks progress through `on_the_way`, `arrived`, `in_progress`, and `completed`. Chat closes when the order is completed or cancelled
- The price shown when you confirm payment is the price that applies to that order

### Cancellation
